What the made-up months showed

! Speed dips on number Stages

Daily WPM fell from about 18 to 13 when the player reached Pulsar Core's number drills, then rose again. Read naively, the line says the player got worse. A raw WPM line mixes easy and hard Stages.

! One key's daily trend is noisy

A key typed only a handful of times on a day swings wildly (see p in B). Per-key trends need a minimum count per point, or a rolling window.

! Gates send players back

Star gates sent the player back to earlier Stages for days, so "the Sector you're in" isn't one band. The prototype colours each day by the furthest Sector reached.
