The whole Path, step by step

A first draft of every Waypoint from the home row to the open-ended speed Sector: its new keys, the drill it opens with, its technique tip, the Recipe its Stages follow, and the Detours that can leave it. The sample texts show the Ladder's shape and are checked against each Waypoint's keys; the real texts are the library's job.

Pace against the month

Bars: where each Sector's first pass ends, at about 5 Runs a Waypoint (4 for a review) and 10 Runs a day. Ticks: the pace targets from The path's shape. The gap is the room for replays toward each Sector's Star total, and for Detours. The real numbers come from A beginner's month, simulated.
